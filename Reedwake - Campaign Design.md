# Reedwake

Reedwake is an eight-gym adventure set in Vaelwater, a region of tidal marshes, quarry
lakes, wind shelves, and a long island chain. The player sets out from Brackenhaven
as an apprentice surveyor. A failing network of sluices and relay stations has cut
the uplands off from the coast. The gym leaders are local specialists, and their
badges mark the routes the player has learned to traverse. Rival surveyor Iona
keeps a competing field log and changes her team after each major watershed.

After the eighth badge, Iona challenges the player at the headwater observatory.
The league at the far end of the Lantern Causeway has four mixed-type teams and
a champion battle at level 63. The credits follow the champion's defeat.

## Regional roster

The three starter lines are planned as Fernut → Bramblet → Canoplate,
Cindrix → Flareden → Vulprism, and Rillfin → Bendruff → Estuarch.
Their final types are Grass/Rock, Fire/Dark, and Water/Fighting, respectively.

The campaign targets 60 original species, including evolutions. Each gym district
has at least one locally obtainable answer to its specialty. All wild encounters,
gifts, trades, fossils, eggs, and trainer parties use regional species in the
finished campaign. The final audit also covers scripted demonstration battles,
postgame encounter tables, and references to legacy names in dialogue.

## Opening prototype

The current source changes update the Brackenhaven entrance town, its sign and
map label, the three starters, and the three early wild species occupying Emerald's
Poochyena, Zigzagoon, and Wurmple slots. Their temporary evolution targets still
use the original game data. The stock opening events and adjacent map connections
are retained at this stage to exercise the starter selection and encounter paths.

The next playable milestone is Brackenhaven through Lockmere, with a new opening
script, dedicated route maps, original early evolutions, an Iona battle, and the
first badge. The remainder of the campaign requires further map, event, sprite,
encounter, and trainer work.
